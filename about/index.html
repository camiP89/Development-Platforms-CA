About Us

Student Express is a student news platform created to share stories, updates and events from the student community. The aim is to provide one place where students can discover what is happening and stay connected with campus life.

The platform includes articles covering topics such as student life, campus events, study-related news and community activities. Registered users can also contribute by creating and publishing their own articles.

Student Express was developed as part of the Development Platforms course at Noroff. The project demonstrates a front-end application using HTML, Tailwind CSS and JavaScript together with Supabase for authentication and database functionality.
